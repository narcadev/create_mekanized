# Changelog

All notable changes to Create: Mekanized are documented here.
The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [0.2.1] - 2026-10-03

### Changed
- The whole mod, including textures and other assets, is now licensed under MIT. Third-party credits moved to NOTICE.md.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-03

### Added
- Andesite Alloy recipe for the Combiner (Andesite + Iron or Zinc Nugget) or the Metallurgic Infuser (8 Andesite + 8 mB Zinc).
- Bronze Ingot recipe for Create's heated mixer (3 Copper Ingots + Tin Ingot -> 4 Bronze).
- Steel Ingot recipe for Create's superheated mixer (Iron Ingot + 2 Coal or Charcoal).
- Create's Millstone and Crushing Wheels can turn Mekanism Clumps into Dirty Dust, and washing turns Dirty Dust into Dust (compatible with Zinc, Iron, Gold, Copper, Osmium, Tin, Lead and Uranium)
- Config sections "Create Recipes in Mekanism", "Mekanism Recipes in Create" and "Create Copies Mekanism Processing" to turn each of these recipe groups on or off.
- Mekanism's Resistive Heater and Fuelwood Heater can heat Create's Basin (not superheated) and Boiler (mirroring a Blaze Burner) while active.
- Added client config.

### Removed
- Enriched Zinc and its recipes: Too strong for early-game progression and is simply replaced by Zinc processing.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-02

### Added
- Zinc processing in Mekanism machines, from 2x up to 5x: Zinc Dust, Dirty Zinc Dust, Zinc Clump, Zinc Shard, Zinc Crystal, and Dirty and Clean Zinc Slurry.
- Enriched Zinc and the Zinc infuse type.
- Create's Crushed Raw Zinc, Iron, Gold, Copper, Osmium, Tin, Lead and Uranium so they can be processed in the Crusher, Chemical Injection Chamber and Chemical Dissolution Chamber.
- Added a config file, including options for Zinc processing.
- Added the Create: Mekanized creative tab.
